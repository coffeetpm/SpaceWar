# Installation-art tone — Interactive light installation

**Goal:** The game feels like an **interactive light installation**, not a battlefield. Calm tension, focused movement, technological art.

---

## Environment tone

- **Minimal space** — Sparse, intentional. No clutter.
- **Geometric light structures** — Beams, grids, defined shapes. Light as architecture.
- **Refraction fields** — Layered prism-like bands, slice trails, clean chromatic edges. Space as a medium.
- **Dark void backgrounds** — Deep blacks and near-blacks. Light reads as the only structure.

---

## Motion language

- **Slow drifting shapes** — Entities and environmental elements drift rather than rush. Deliberate, not frantic.
- **Controlled energy pulses** — Beams, charges, and reactions are timed and bounded. Rhythmic, not chaotic.
- **Rhythmic environmental reactions** — Background and atmosphere respond to events in a measured way (e.g. opacity, subtle motion). Installation responds to the visitor.

---

## Combat tone

- **Clean, precise, sculptural.** Interactions read as **light cutting through light**, not violence.
- **Enemies feel like:**
  - **Moving constructs** — Geometric forms in space.
  - **Geometric entities** — Defined shapes, clear silhouettes.
  - **Light-driven machines** — Energy signatures, not organic life.

**Not:** soldiers, monsters, war units. No military or horror framing in shape or motion.

**Enemy visual spec:** Polygon forms, rotating segments, energy cores, refraction surfaces. Smooth, intentional, sculptural motion. See **docs/ENEMY_VISUAL_IDENTITY.md**.

---

## Visual emotion

| Target | Avoid |
|--------|--------|
| **Calm tension** | Panic, chaos |
| **Focused movement** | Frantic dodging, explosion spam |
| **Technological art** | Battlefield, war game, horror |
